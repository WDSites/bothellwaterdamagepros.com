Kitchen water damage in Bothell homes usually hides inside the cabinets

.

Kitchen water damage restoration in Bothell, WA is about one room where water goes unseen for a long time. Leaks start under the sink, behind the dishwasher or at the refrigerator ice line, then spread into cabinet boxes, toe-kicks and the floor under them.

This page covers the room itself and the decisions about what stays and what comes out. If the appliance is the question, see appliance leak water damage. For the rebuild after drying, see water damage repair.

First-hour safety steps in a wet kitchen

.

While you wait for a crew

  • Close the small shut-off valve under the sink or at the appliance. If you cannot find it, close the main valve for the house.
  • Unplug the dishwasher and refrigerator only if you can reach the outlet without standing in water. Otherwise turn off the kitchen circuit at the panel.
  • Do not use outlets near the sink while the counter and floor are wet.
  • Move small items off the floor and photograph the cabinets before opening anything.
  • Call a plumber for the repair. Washington L&I says only certified plumbers can legally do plumbing work on someone else's property, while owners may work on their own.

Cabinets, countertops and what is worth keeping

.

Particleboard swells when it gets wet and rarely shrinks back. Plywood boxes and solid wood doors usually dry well. Many countertops sit on a particleboard substrate that can swell at the sink cutout even when the surface looks fine.

The island deserves its own look. Island cabinets often sit directly on the floor with the dishwasher or sink on one side, so water can get under the whole unit. We do not remove a cabinet unless the readings and the material say it has to go.

Floors under and around the cabinets
Floor typeWhat mattersTypical decision
Vinyl or linoleum.Water gets under seams and sits on the subfloor.Lift it if the subfloor reads wet.
Tile.Tile survives, but grout, mortar and backer may hold water.Check the substrate, dry in place when readings allow.
Hardwood.Cupping appears within days and heat can damage it.Dry with caution or replace boards.
Laminate or click vinyl.Edges swell and rarely recover.Usually replaced.

The EPA's guidance for water-damaged buildings says hard porous flooring such as vinyl, linoleum and tile can be cleaned, but the underflooring needs to be checked and dried. It also says to use caution when applying heat to hardwood floors.

Insurance and older kitchens

.

The Washington Office of the Insurance Commissioner says home policies are meant for sudden events and often exclude leaks that continued for a long time. A drip behind a cabinet for months may be treated differently from a failed line today. We do not promise coverage.

Remodel work can disturb old flooring and adhesives. The Puget Sound Clean Air Agency says to check for asbestos before starting any renovation project, so mention the age of the house when you call.

Prevention and mistakes to avoid

.
  • Leaving a wet toe-kick in place because the floor looks dry.
  • Painting or caulking over a stained backsplash wall.
  • Running a fan across wet cabinets with no dehumidifier.
  • Replacing the cabinets before the source is fixed.
