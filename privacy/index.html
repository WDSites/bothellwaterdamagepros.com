Who we are

Bothell Water Damage Pros operates this website and a 24/7 emergency dispatch line for water damage and mold restoration in Bothell, Kenmore, Woodinville, Kirkland and Lynnwood, Washington.

Our address is 18012 Bothell Everett Hwy, Bothell, WA 98012. You can reach us at (425) 592-3399 or contact@bothellwaterdamagepros.com.

Information we receive when you call

When you call (425) 592-3399, we receive your phone number and the details you choose to share, such as the address of the loss, what happened and when it started.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the person who answers and ask to end the call.

We use call details to understand the problem, send a restoration professional when one is available, give you an explanation of scope and price before work starts, and follow up on the call you made.

Who may receive your details

Restoration work is carried out by licensed and insured restoration professionals. To dispatch a crew, we share your name, phone number and the address of the loss with the professional assigned to your job.

We do not publish your details, and we do not use this site to collect payment card numbers or government identification numbers. Please do not read those out on a call unless you are paying a professional directly.

Website visits and cookies

This site does not ask you to create an account and does not use forms. We do not set advertising cookies from our own pages.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and, lower on each page, a Google Maps embed. Those services receive standard connection data such as your IP address and browser details, and they may set their own cookies under their own policies.

How long we keep information

We keep call details and recordings for as long as we need them to handle your job, answer questions about it and meet any record-keeping duty. After that we delete them or remove identifying details.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it by emailing contact@bothellwaterdamagepros.com or calling (425) 592-3399. We will answer as soon as we reasonably can.

Washington residents may have additional rights under state law. If you want to use one, email us and say which right you mean.

Children

This site is meant for property owners and managers. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information we will update this page and the date at the top.
