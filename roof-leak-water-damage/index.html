Where roof water ends up inside a Bothell house

.

Roof leak water damage in Bothell, WA is what the water does after it gets past the roof: wet attic insulation, a stained or sagging ceiling, damp wall cavities and, in a bad case, water running down inside an exterior wall to the floor. We dry and clean the house. A roofer fixes the roof, and the two jobs should happen close together.

Rain here is mostly steady rather than violent, which suits slow leaks. A failed flashing, a cracked vent boot or a skylight curb can drip for weeks before anything shows below.

Local note.

Why winter finds the weak spot in a roof

.

The National Weather Service technical memorandum on the climate of Seattle says more than 75 percent of the yearly precipitation falls in the wet season, which it defines as October through March. NOAA's 1991 to 2020 normals for the Seattle-Tacoma station put November at 6.31 inches, against 0.60 inches in July.

A flashing gap that stays dry all summer can start to leak with the first long autumn rain, and moss and clogged gutters then hold water against the roof edge.

Roof leak safety steps before help arrives

.
  • If the ceiling is bulging, stay out from under it, set a bucket nearby and call. Water pooled above drywall can bring the ceiling down.
  • Move electronics out of the room.
  • If water is near a light fixture, ceiling fan or outlet, turn that circuit off at the panel only if the panel is dry. Otherwise stay out and call your electric utility or 911.
  • Do not climb onto a wet roof. Photograph the stain and anything damaged from inside.

What we do and what a roofer does

.

We do not repair roofs, replace flashing, clean gutters or install tarps. That work belongs to a licensed roofer, and the sooner the roofer stops the water, the smaller our job becomes. Our job is the building below the roof. We find where the water went, remove what cannot be saved and dry what can.

Moisture mapping

A moisture meter and a thermal camera show how far the water traveled across the ceiling and into the walls.

Open and remove

We open the ceiling where it is wet or sagging and remove soaked insulation and drywall.

Drying

Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers run on the affected rooms, with daily moisture readings.

Documentation

Photos and a written scope are prepared for your insurer.

Drying follows ANSI/IICRC S500, the published standard for professional water damage restoration. We cite it as a reference and do not claim a firm certification under it. For the wider process see water mitigation, and for what happens after drying see water damage repair.

Insurance and permits

.

The Washington Office of the Insurance Commissioner says that if a storm damages your roof, you need to fix the damage quickly to stop more water from entering, and that insurance will not cover water damage if you delay repairs. It also says most home policies do not cover gradual leaks. Whether your roof leak is covered is your insurer's decision, and we do not promise coverage.

The City of Bothell lists a re-roof application for single-family homes that is issued over the counter once the contractor's city and state licenses are verified. The city exempts in-kind overlays on one and two-family homes that result in no more than two layers. Ask your roofer which applies to your repair.

Preventing the next leak

.
  • Clear gutters and downspouts before the October rains.
  • Remove moss that lifts shingle edges, and have flashing at chimneys and skylights checked.
  • Do not paint over a ceiling stain. It hides a leak that may still be active.
