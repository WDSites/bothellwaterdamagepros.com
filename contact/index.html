What to have ready when you call

  • The address of the loss and a good callback number.
  • What happened, and whether the water source has been stopped.
  • Which rooms and floors are wet, and roughly how long the water has been there.
  • Whether the water looks clean, dirty or like sewage.
  • Your insurer's name, if you have already reported the loss.
