Downtown Bothell between the ridges and the river

.

The city's Downtown plan calls this the historic center of Bothell, nestled between forested hills along the Sammamish River. The core is an L-shaped area around Main Street and SR 527. The south edge is the Sammamish River corridor and the east edge is the UW Bothell and Cascadia campus.

City Hall at 18415 101st Avenue NE and the Park at Bothell Landing use ZIP code 98011. The city pages we read do not state the county for Downtown, so this page leaves it out.

Who lives Downtown

.

The city says the larger multifamily buildings in Bothell are mostly concentrated Downtown, and its plan expects townhomes, duplexes and live-work buildings in the transition district. The Sunrise and Valley View District is described as two mostly single-family areas with quiet streets and mixed housing types. Valley View Road near 108th Avenue NE has a multifamily cluster on sloping parcels.

.

What the city and county documents say

.

The Sammamish River is the most prominent physical feature of the Downtown subarea, which the plan describes as enclosed by steep ridges on three sides and the river to the south. Wetlands along North Creek lie at the campus edge. A King County appendix says the river channel was dredged and straightened in the 1960s, cutting it off from its floodplain.

The Downtown plan calls for a curb on Main Street to prevent flooding of adjacent storefronts. That is a design note, not a recorded flood. The city's older hazard plan lists chronic floodwater and drainage trouble citywide in wet winters, and says the Bothell Landing amphitheater has flooded numerous times with no damage associated with the flooding.

Source type: City of Bothell Downtown plan and hazard mitigation plan, King County flood planning documents.

For a building, that points to slope seepage and slow drains as the questions to ask, along with plumbing and roof leaks.

FEMA flood status and storm history for Downtown

.

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. Look up your own address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center. Standard homeowners policies usually exclude flood, so ask your insurer.

Citywide, about 6.10 percent of Bothell's land is in special flood hazard areas. The December 2007 storm was a regional event and the sources do not tie it to Downtown buildings. Apart from the amphitheater, no flood damage in Downtown homes or shops was found in the public sources checked.

Scenarios we expect in Downtown buildings

.

Multi-unit leaks

A burst supply line or failed water heater in an upper unit can wet the ceilings, walls and floors of several neighbors. See burst pipe water damage and water heater leak cleanup.

Shops and mixed-use buildings

Storefronts and apartments over retail share pipes and floors. See commercial water damage.

Slope seepage in Sunrise and Valley View

Sloping parcels and steep ridges let rain collect below grade, which can wet lower floors and garages.

Safety before anyone starts cleanup

.
  • Stop the source if it is safe, and tell neighbors and the manager if water may move between units.
  • Do not enter flooded areas. Do not operate breakers or touch electrical items while standing in water, or if water reached outlets or panels.
  • Sewage and river water are contaminated, so no do-it-yourself drying or cleanup of those.
  • Photograph everything first. Do not assume all is lost or all can be saved.

How a crew handles a multi-unit loss

.

A crew extracts standing water, then uses moisture meters and a thermal camera to follow water through walls, ceilings and floors, including adjoining units. Air movers and dehumidifiers dry what can dry. Materials that cannot dry are removed, and the work is documented. If materials stay wet, mold can grow, so drying quickly matters. See mold remediation for that scope.

Records for the insurer and the building

.

Keep dated photos, receipts, moisture readings and notes on what was removed. In a condo or apartment, the association and several insurers may be involved, so ask yours what applies. Calls from Downtown are part of our service to Bothell.
