Where Westhill sits, and why the county line matters

.

Westhill is a formal Bothell subarea of about 701 acres, an upland land mass on the west side that slopes down to SR 522 at its south end. Streets include NE 180th, NE 188th, NE 191st, 88th Avenue NE and 92nd Avenue NE. Bothell High School and Westhill Elementary are here.

It is mostly in King County, with a small portion in Snohomish County. Not all of it was inside city limits under the 2015 plan, which describes an unincorporated southern area between NE 178th Street and SR 522. The current extent of those pockets was not verified, so do not assume your address is in the city.

This is not the West Hill near Skyway, a separate place. The plan describes mostly detached single-family homes at roughly four to six per acre, with multifamily limited to a narrow strip north of SR 522. Some partly developed properties were still on septic.

What the city's plan says about water in Westhill

.

The west part drains to the Swamp Creek sub-basin and the east part to the Horse Creek sub-basin. Little Swamp Creek crosses the northwest portion and flows year-round into Swamp Creek, starting at wetlands east of 9th Avenue SE.

Ground falls from about 268 feet to about 20 feet or less near the Sammamish River. Slopes over 15 percent run along the south end behind the SR 522 commercial properties. Runoff moves through catch basins, pipes and ditches.

Source type: City of Bothell subarea plan.

A year-round creek and wetlands mean saturated ground after a wet stretch, and a steep slope sends water toward whatever sits below. The city's surface water page says stormwater standards were revised for the Little Swamp Creek basin after Kenmore flooding, and it names the basin, not Westhill.

The city's hazard plan reports landslides in southwest Bothell, south of SR 522 and west of I-405, and does not name Westhill in the text checked. No flood or landslide history tied to Westhill was found in the public sources checked.

Flood maps for Westhill addresses

What goes wrong in Westhill homes

.

Near the creek and below the south slope, groundwater and runoff can reach a lower level, as covered on basement water damage cleanup. Among single-family roofs, a ceiling stain after a storm points to roof leak water damage.

Sewer providers differ. The plan says the city sewers parts inside its boundary, Northshore Utility District served the unincorporated southern area, and Alderwood Water and Wastewater District serves the Snohomish County portion. See sewage backup cleanup for the cleanup side. Edges of Westhill touch Norway Hill and Downtown Bothell.

Westhill water damage safety: the first steps

.
  1. Stop the source if you can do it safely.
  2. Do not enter flooded rooms or basements.
  3. Do not operate breakers or touch electrical equipment while standing in water, or if water reached outlets or the panel.
  4. Sewage and creek water are contaminated, so no DIY drying or cleanup.
  5. Photograph before moving anything.
  6. Do not assume everything is lost, and do not assume all of it can be rescued.

What the drying work involves

.

A crew extracts standing water, then maps moisture with meters and a thermal camera to find wet framing and subfloor. Air movers and dehumidifiers run until readings come down, and materials that cannot dry are removed. Water mitigation describes the stage.

If materials stay wet, mold can grow, so drying quickly matters. See mold remediation for that question.

Records your insurer may ask for

.

Keep dated photos, receipts, moisture readings and notes on what was removed. Ask your insurer what applies to your policy.
